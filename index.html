Interactive SWOT Analysis Tool

A simple framework for analyzing business problems and making strategic decisions.

SWOT stands for Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats. It helps organizations understand their current situation before making important decisions.

What is SWOT Analysis?

SWOT is a business strategy framework used to evaluate a situation before making decisions. It categorizes factors into two main types: internal and external.

Strengths and Weaknesses are internal factors - things within your organization's control. Opportunities and Threats are external factors - things outside your organization's control that can affect your success.

Strengths

Internal Factor

Strengths are the advantages your organization already has. These can include resources, capabilities, experienced employees, strong reputation, or efficient systems.

Example: A company may have strong customer data or highly skilled employees.

Weaknesses

Internal Factor

Weaknesses are internal limitations or areas where the organization struggles or needs improvement.

Example: Outdated technology or lack of experience with new tools.

Opportunities

External Factor

Opportunities are external situations or trends that could help the organization grow or improve.

Example: New markets, emerging technologies, or increasing customer demand.

Threats

External Factor

Threats are external risks that could harm the organization.

Example: New competitors, economic downturns, or regulatory changes.

How SWOT Helps Decision Making

SWOT analysis helps leaders make better strategic decisions by providing a comprehensive view of their situation:

  1. Look at what your organization does well (Strengths)
  2. Identify internal limitations (Weaknesses)
  3. Look for outside opportunities (Opportunities)
  4. Identify outside risks (Threats)

By examining all four areas together, organizations can develop strategies that leverage their strengths, address weaknesses, capitalize on opportunities, and mitigate threats.

Example SWOT Scenario

Problem: A company is considering adopting AI tools to improve customer support.

  • Strength: Strong customer data and experienced support team
  • Weakness: Limited experience with AI technology
  • Opportunity: AI could automate simple customer questions and reduce response time
  • Threat: Poor AI responses could damage customer trust
